Major Project · AI / Digital Forensics

Explainable AI for Deepfake Voice Detection

An explainable deep-learning system that distinguishes genuine speech from AI-generated audio, and shows its reasoning instead of a black-box verdict. Built with two teammates as a final-year major project.

  • CNN + BiLSTM + attention architecture over mel-spectrogram & MFCC features
  • Grad-CAM and SHAP-based explainability layer
  • Deployed as a Flask web application with chunk-level analysis
